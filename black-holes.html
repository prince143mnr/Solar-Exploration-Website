ASTROPHYSICAL ANATOMY

STRUCTURE OF A BLACK HOLE

Five essential components described by Einstein's equations and quantum mechanics.

1. THE EVENT HORIZON

The boundary of no return. Defined by the Schwarzschild radius \(R_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}\). Once matter or light crosses this threshold, all light cones tip inward toward the singularity.

2. THE SINGULARITY

At the dead center lies a mathematical zero-volume point of infinite spacetime curvature and infinite density, where General Relativity breaks down and quantum gravity is needed.

3. THE ACCRETION DISK

Plasma spiraling inwards at up to 30% the speed of light. Viscous friction heats the matter to tens of millions of degrees, generating violent X-ray radiation.

4. GRAVITATIONAL LENSING

Extreme spacetime warping bends photon trajectories, allowing observers on Earth to see light from stars and the accretion disk located directly behind the black hole.
