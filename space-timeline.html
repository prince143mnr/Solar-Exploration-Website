THE GRAND CHRONOLOGY

THE SPACE TIMELINE

From the initial Planck epoch of the Big Bang to the formation of stars, human space exploration, and our destiny across the interstellar horizon.

13.8 BILLION YEARS AGO

THE BIG BANG & COSMIC INFLATION

Spacetime, energy, and fundamental physical laws originate from a gravitational singularity. In the first fraction of a second, cosmic inflation expands the universe exponentially by a factor of at least \(10^{26}\).

13.6 BILLION YEARS AGO

POPULATION III STARS & REIONIZATION

The first gargantuan stars of pure primordial hydrogen and helium ignite, ending the Cosmic Dark Ages and seeding the universe with the first heavy chemical elements through hypernovae.

4.6 BILLION YEARS AGO

FORMATION OF THE SUN

A dense interstellar molecular cloud collapses, forming our proto-Sun. The leftover accretion disc forms a spinning protoplanetary nebula where dust grains collide and coalesce into planetesimals.

4.5 BILLION YEARS AGO

EARTH & THE THEIA IMPACT

Proto-Earth collides with a Mars-sized planet named Theia. Ejected molten debris coalesces in orbit to form the Moon, stabilizing Earth's axial tilt and generating the tidal rhythms that drive marine life.

1609 CE

GALILEO'S TELESCOPE

Galileo Galilei points an optical spyglass toward the night sky, discovering the four major moons of Jupiter (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto), dismantling the geocentric model of the universe forever.

1957 CE

SPUTNIK 1 & THE DAWN OF THE SPACE AGE

The first artificial orbital satellite is placed into Low Earth Orbit by the Soviet Union, broadcasting radio pulses across the globe and launching the international Space Race.

1969 CE

APOLLO 11: FOOTSTEPS ON THE MOON

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to stand on another world. "One small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

1977 CE

VOYAGER 1 VOYAGE TO INTERSTELLAR SPACE

Launched to conduct flybys of Jupiter and Saturn, Voyager 1 goes on to become the farthest human-made object from Earth, crossing the heliopause in 2012 into the open interstellar void.

2021 CE

JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE AT L2

The 6.5m infrared flagship deploys flawlessly at Sun-Earth L2, analyzing exoplanet atmospheres for water vapor and detecting galaxies that formed less than 300 million years after the Big Bang.

2026+ CE

ARTEMIS LUNAR BASE & MARS VOYAGES

Permanent human scientific habitation established at the Moon's South Pole to harvest ice. First crewed interplanetary voyages launched toward the Martian red sands.

DEEP FUTURE

INTERSTELLAR HUMANITY & DYSON SWARMS

Harnessing solar power through megastructures, autonomous laser-sail probes journeying to Proxima Centauri b, and human civilization taking root across multiple star systems.
