SAGITTARIUS A*

The supermassive black hole at the dynamical center of the Milky Way galaxy. Imaged in millimeter wavelengths by the Event Horizon Telescope, unveiling a glowing ring formed by photons orbiting the black hole shadow.

EVENT HORIZON TELESCOPE OBSERVATION

Using Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI) across eight synchronized radio observatories spanning Antarctica, Chile, Hawaii, and Europe, astronomers created an Earth-sized virtual telescope. In 2022, the first image of Sagittarius A* was revealed, confirming Einstein's General Relativity with extraordinary precision.

RELATIVISTIC BEAMING & SHADOW

Photons emitted by gas revolving toward Earth are amplified by relativistic Doppler brightening, causing one side of the ring to shine brighter than the other. The central dark shadow is roughly 2.6 times the diameter of the Schwarzschild radius due to strong gravitational bending of incoming and outgoing light rays.
