PROJECT MANIFESTO

THE VISION BEHIND COSMOS

An ultra-premium, cinematic space exploration observatory bridging scientific precision, Interstellar cinema, and futuristic interactive engineering.

PILLAR 01

SCIENTIFIC DOCUMENTARY DEPTH

Every planetary metric, orbital distance, escape velocity, and atmospheric composition is drawn directly from NASA JPL Horizons telemetry, ESA surveys, and peer-reviewed astrophysical literature.

PILLAR 02

INTERSTELLAR CINEMA & POLISH

Designed with 7-layer parallax universes, relativistic Doppler accretion disks, cosmic noise textures, and Apple-grade 3D card tilt physics, providing visitors with an authentic sensation of deep-space voyage.

PILLAR 03

HIGH-PERFORMANCE VANILLA ARCHITECTURE

Built entirely in pure semantic HTML5, modern modular CSS3, and hardware-accelerated Vanilla JavaScript. Utilizing the Web Audio API for zero-dependency space drone synthesis with zero external framework overhead.

ASTRONOMICAL DATA & MISSION REPOSITORIES

The telemetry, trajectories, and spectral metrics across COSMOS are indexed from the world's leading astronomical institutions:

NASA JET PROPULSION LABORATORY Planetary ephemerides, rover navigation logs, and Voyager interstellar telemetry.
EVENT HORIZON TELESCOPE COLLABORATION Sub-millimeter interferometry imaging of Sagittarius A* and Messier 87*.
SPACE TELESCOPE SCIENCE INSTITUTE (STScI) Hubble and James Webb Space Telescope deep-field infrared spectroscopy.
EUROPEAN SPACE AGENCY (ESA) Gaia mission astrometry mapping 1.8 billion stellar positions and motions.
